# AI Tutor for Learning Complex Topics
## Use this skill when
- You need to learn a difficult subject.
- You want an adaptive learning experience tailored to your knowledge level.
- You prefer interactive explanations over static content.

## What it is
This skill enables an AI agent to act as a tutor, assessing your current understanding, planning lessons based on your needs, and providing step-by-step guidance. It adapts the teaching approach based on your responses and reasoning, ensuring you build a solid foundation before moving on to more complex concepts.

## Steps
1. **Install LearnHarness**: Download and install LearnHarness from [learnharness.ai](https://learnharness.ai) or GitHub releases.
2. **Configure Models**: Set up your preferred models using provider API keys or supported subscription connections.
3. **Start a Session**: Begin a learning session by specifying the topic you want to understand.
4. **Measure Understanding**: The agent evaluates your current knowledge level on various aspects of the topic.
5. **Plan Lessons**: Develops a dependency map from your current understanding to your learning goals.
6. **Teach and Practice**: Engage in interactive lessons where you predict, receive explanations, and check your understanding. Practice sessions reinforce learned concepts with varied problems.

## Checks
- Ensure LearnHarness is installed correctly on your machine.
- Verify that the configured models are accessible and properly set up.
- Confirm that the learning session starts without errors and adapts to your responses.

## Pitfalls
- Incorrect model configuration can lead to suboptimal teaching sessions.
- Lack of internet connectivity may affect real-time research or interactive features.
- Over-reliance on AI explanations might hinder independent thinking development.
