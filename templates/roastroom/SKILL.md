# RoastRoom Code Idea Roasting Skill

## Use this skill when
- You need to give blunt but fair feedback on coding ideas.
- A team member proposes an innovative solution that requires clarification or improvement.
- The goal is to maintain a fun and engaging atmosphere while ensuring code quality.

## What it is
This skill involves delivering constructive criticism in a humorous manner, inspired by the RoastRoom platform's style. It aims to encourage creativity and learning without discouraging participants.

## Steps
1. **Listen Actively**: Understand the proposed idea or solution thoroughly.
2. **Identify Strengths and Weaknesses**: Break down the code or idea into its components and assess both its merits and potential pitfalls.
3. **Formulate a Blunt yet Constructive Response**:
   - Start with a positive aspect to set a friendly tone.
   - Transition smoothly into areas needing improvement, using humor to soften the critique.
   - Provide specific examples and suggestions for enhancement.
4. **Deliver the Feedback**: Share your thoughts clearly and concisely, ensuring the recipient understands both the praise and the constructive criticism.

## Checks
- Ensure the feedback is balanced and not overly negative.
- Verify that humor does not overshadow the importance of the critique.
- Confirm that the recipient feels motivated to improve rather than discouraged.

## Pitfalls
- Avoid being too harsh or mean-spirited, even in jest.
- Be mindful of cultural differences in humor; what works for one person might not for another.
- Ensure that the feedback is actionable and provides clear paths forward.
